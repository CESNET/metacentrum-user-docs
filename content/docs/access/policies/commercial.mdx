---
title: Rules of Commercial Use of Computing Resources Provided through MetaCentrum
---

1. MetaCentrum cannot make its own resources available on a commercial basis, and excludes any liability regarding the availability or quality of its services.
2. MetaCentrum resources are primarily intended for non-commercial use in research, development and education; licensing conditions for most of the available application software packages do not even allow for any other types of use.
3. Commercial use of computing resources provided through MetaCentrum must adhere to rules laid out below.
4. Commercial use of resources provided through MetaCentrum is possible exclusively upon agreement between the user and the computing centre whose resources are to be used commercially.
5. Resources for commercial use must be accessed separately. For that purpose, a separate VO will be created in Perun upon the user’s request, adhering to conditions stated by the MetaCentrum Administrator.
6. MetaCentrum must be notified of the commercial use case.
