---
title: Rules of Commercial Use of MetaCentrum Services
---
Version 1.0. This policy is effective from 1 January 2027.

1. Commercial use of MetaCentrum is permitted but limited to hardware resources eligible for commercial activities and software packages with licenses allowing commercial use.

0. All commercial activities require prior approval based on a submitted specification that ensures clear separation from non-commercial use. It must specify in particular the time frame, involved users, processed data, and utilized resources.

0. MetaCentrum excludes any liability regarding the availability or quality of its services.
